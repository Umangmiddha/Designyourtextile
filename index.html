Why DYT

A design studio that speaks fabric - and shows it in motion

Textile businesses need more than a pretty pattern. A design has to repeat, print and produce - and then it has to be sold. We pair production-grade textile artwork with 3D visualization and motion design, so the same studio carries your idea from file to shelf.

01

Built Around the Repeat

Every print begins as a true seamless tile. We build half-drop, brick, mirror and tossed repeats that tile invisibly at production scale - no seams, no drift, no surprises on the fabric roll.

02

Seen Before It's Made

3D CAD visualization turns flat artwork into a realistic digital twin - fabric drape, garment and home product renders, material and colourway studies - so decisions happen before a single yard is sampled.

03

Built to Sell, Not Just Print

Motion design brings fabric to life - animated drape and flow, print repeat motion, product turntables and collection showreels that turn technical work into something buyers and marketing teams can run with.

How We Work

From first moodboard to approved strike-off and showreel

A studio workflow that keeps design intent intact through 3D approval, sampling, colour sign-off and bulk production.

  1. 01

    Brief & Fabric Research

    We start with the end use - fabric type, construction, weight, market, season and price point - then research trends, references and competitor direction before drawing a single motif.

  2. 02

    Concept & Motif Development

    Hand sketches, painted motifs and digital experiments come together into concept boards, with early repeats and colour direction proposed for review.

  3. 03

    Repeat, Colourway & 3D Build

    Chosen directions are built into true seamless repeats and mapped across colourways - then visualized in 3D CAD so the design can be seen on fabric, garment or product before sampling.

  4. 04

    Sampling & Strike-Off Review

    We work alongside mills and printers on strike-offs, adjusting colour, scale and repeat until the physical sample matches the approved design and render.

  5. 05

    Motion & Production Handover

    Final layered files, production artwork, tech packs and colour standards are packaged for bulk run - alongside motion assets that carry the collection to market.
