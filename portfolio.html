FAQ

Textile questions, answered

The things brands, mills and buyers usually ask before we begin.

What kind of studio is DYT?

DYT is a graphic design studio specialized in textile businesses. We combine textile design, production-ready artwork, 3D CAD visualization and motion design - so a fabric concept can be designed, visualized, animated and produced under one roof.

What does a textile design and artwork project deliver?

Typically seamless repeat tiles, layered source files, colour-separated production files, colourway variants, mill-ready tech packs, strike-off artwork and repeat metadata such as repeat size in cm, DPI and Pantone references.

What is 3D CAD visualization for textiles?

It turns a flat design into a realistic digital twin - fabric drape, garment or home product mockups, material and colourway renders - so the look can be approved before anything is sampled.

What does motion design include?

Animated drape and flow, print repeat motion, product turntables and collection showreels - motion assets built for launches, e-commerce and social, all from the same designs and 3D models.

How do we start a project?

Email admin@designyourtextile.com with your fabric type, product category, season, quantity, target market and timeline. We reply within one business day with a scope and next steps.
