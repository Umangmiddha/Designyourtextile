What to include in your brief

  • Fabric type — e.g. cotton lawn, viscose, denim, single jersey, upholstery woven.
  • Service needed — textile design, production-ready artwork, 3D CAD visualization, or motion design.
  • Product & market — the end product, target customer and price point.
  • Season & quantity — the season you're designing for and expected production volume.
  • Timeline — key dates such as sampling, approval and bulk start.
  • References — any mood, colour, CAD or motion references you'd like us to see.

Before You Send

A few quick answers

How soon will I hear back?

Within one business day. Every enquiry goes to admin@designyourtextile.com and is reviewed personally.

What happens after I send my brief?

We review the brief, ask any clarifying questions, and come back with a proposed scope, approach and timeline - whether that's design, production artwork, 3D CAD, motion, or a combination. If it's a fit, we agree terms and begin with the research stage.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work with brands, mills and labels worldwide and coordinate with suppliers in different time zones as part of the sampling, 3D review and handover stages.
