The Studio

A graphic design studio built around one industry

DYT - Design Your Textile - is a graphic design studio specialized in textile businesses. We don't split our attention across packaging, FMCG or general branding. Everything we do serves cloth and the people who make and sell it: textile design, production-ready artwork, 3D CAD visualization and motion design.

Why Textile Only

Specialists beat generalists in textile

Textile has its own rules. A repeat has to tile invisibly. A colour has to survive the dye bath. A design has to be produced, then shown to a buyer who was never in the room. When a studio works across many industries, these rules get diluted - and the presentation gets generic. We chose to work in one industry so we could master its rules and its language.

That focus means we think in repeat sizes, not artboards. In Pantone and dyelots, not hex codes. And because we also work in 3D CAD and motion, we can carry the same design from a flat file to a rendered, animated presentation - without ever handing it to a different studio.

What We Handle

The full textile design brief

The things a design team - or a single designer - has to take care of on a textile project. We cover all of it, and document it so nothing gets lost downstream.

Research

Trend & Market Research

Seasonal direction, colour forecasting, competitor and category research, and reference gathering before a single motif is drawn.

Design

Motif & Pattern Creation

Hand-painted and digital motif development, composition, layout and the transition from artwork into a true repeat.

Production

Prepress & Production Artwork

Production-resolution files, colour separation, ICC guidance and repeat metadata prepared for digital, rotary or flat-bed printing.

3D

3D CAD Visualization

Fabric, garment and home product models with realistic drape, material and colourway renders - built to CAD precision for approval and catalogue use.

Motion

Motion Design

Drape and flow animation, print repeat motion, product turntables and collection showreels built for launches, e-commerce and social.

Handover

Tech Packs & Documentation

Structured tech packs, spec sheets and file organisation so the mill, printer and garment team all work from the same truth.

Tools & Craft

Traditional hand, modern production

We work by hand where it matters and digitally where it counts - from paint and pencil to 3D CAD and motion. The aim is always the same: original design that runs cleanly in production and presents powerfully on screen.

By Hand

Painted & Drawn

Gouache, ink, watercolour and pencil work - the texture and imperfection that digital tools alone rarely capture.

Digital

Repeat & Colour

Seamless repeat building, colour separation, colourway mapping and production-resolution artwork preparation.

3D CAD

Modelling & Rendering

Fabric, garment and product models with realistic drape, material and lighting - rendered across colourways and variants.

Motion

Animation & Showreels

Drape animation, print motion, product turntables and edited collection showreels built from the same designs and 3D models.

Who We Work With

Brands, mills and labels

We work with fashion labels, home textile brands, mills and printers, and independent designers who need production-grade artwork - and the 3D and motion that sell it.

Fashion & apparel labels

Seasonal print and structure design, colourways, repeats, production files and 3D garment renders for womenswear, menswear and kidswear across woven and knit fabrics.

Home & furnishing brands

Upholstery, drapery, bedding and cushion design - placement prints, wovens and jacquards, visualized in 3D and presented in motion for interiors.

Mills, printers & converters

Production-ready artwork and repeat engineering, plus 3D visualization and motion to help mills present their own seasonal collections or client-specific ranges.

Independent designers & startups

End-to-end design, documentation, 3D renders and motion for smaller runs - helping new labels get production-grade files and presentation-ready assets without building an in-house team.
