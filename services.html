Our Services

Everything a textile business needs before and after the loom

DYT covers the full journey of a textile idea - design, production artwork, 3D CAD visualization and motion. Each service is built to hand off cleanly to a mill, printer, buyer or marketing team, with no translation lost between screen and shelf.

Service 01

Textile Design

Hand-painted and digital surface patterns, engineered into true seamless repeats and prepared for reactive, pigment, disperse or digital printing.

What we make

Surface Patterns

Florals, geometrics, abstract marks, conversationals, textures and stripes - developed from hand-painted motifs and refined digitally into production-ready artwork.

  • Hand paint
  • Digital motif
  • Texture
Repeat engineering

Seamless Repeats

Half-drop, brick, mirror, tossed and full-drop repeats built to tile invisibly at production scale, with repeat size documented in centimetres and DPI.

  • Half-drop
  • Mirror
  • Brick
  • Tossed
Placement

Engineered & Placement Prints

Prints engineered to a garment panel, cushion outline or border - with repeat-safe edges, panel sizing and directional notes for cutting and sewing.

  • Panels
  • Borders
  • Placement
Output

Colourways & Separations

Each print delivered across multiple colourways with Pantone references and colour separations - so the design survives the dye bath and the print run.

  • Pantone
  • Colour separations
  • Dyelot
Service 02

Production-Ready Artwork

The unglamorous work that decides whether a design actually reaches the shelf. We document and prepare everything a mill, printer or garment team needs.

Documentation

Textile Tech Packs

Repeat size, colourway references, construction notes, yarn and substrate specs, placement diagrams and finishing instructions - all in one structured pack.

Prepress

Colour Separation & Files

Separated, production-resolution files with ICC guidance and colour-managed output for digital, rotary or flat-bed textile printing.

Sampling

Strike-Off & Sample Support

We review strike-offs and lab dips with you, adjusting colour, scale and repeat until the physical sample matches the approved design.

Files

Layered Source & Spec

Editable layered source files in PSD, AI or TIFF at production resolution, with motifs, repeats and colour layers kept separate and clearly organised.

Service 03

3D CAD Visualization

Turn a flat design into a realistic digital twin. We model and render fabric, garments and home products in 3D CAD so the design can be seen, approved and presented before anything is sampled.

Material

Fabric & Material Renders

Realistic cloth drape, weave and knit texture, sheen and lighting - rendered so a print or structure reads true to the finished fabric.

  • Drape
  • Texture
  • Material
Product

Garment & Home 3D Models

3D CAD models of garments, cushions, throws, upholstery and accessories with your design applied, ready for buyer and catalogue approval.

  • Apparel
  • Home
  • Accessories
Study

Colourway & Variant Renders

Every colourway and scale variation rendered from the same model, so ranges can be reviewed side-by-side without sampling each option.

  • Colourways
  • Scale
  • Variants
Service 04

Motion Design

Bring fabric and product to life. We animate drape, flow and print motion, and cut collection showreels that turn technical work into something buyers and marketing teams can run with.

Fabric

Drape & Flow Animation

Animated cloth behaviour - movement, fold, stretch and flow - so the hand and drape of a fabric can be understood on screen.

Pattern

Print & Repeat Motion

Repeats, placements and engineered prints set in motion - showing scale, tiling and colour movement as a living pattern rather than a static tile.

Product

Product Turntables & Reels

Rotating 3D product sequences and short loops built from the same CAD models - ideal for launches, e-commerce and social.

Brand

Collection Showreels

A single edited showreel bringing a season or range together - designs, renders and motion cut into one presentation for buyers and trade.

Deliverables

What lands in your folder

Every project is handed over in an organised, production-ready package - no chasing, no missing layers, no guesswork for the mill or the marketing team.

Files

Layered Source

Editable layered artwork in PSD, AI or TIFF at production resolution, with motifs, repeats and colour layers kept separate.

Production

Print / Loom Art

Separated production files with repeat metadata, DPI notes and colour standards matched to your print or weave method.

3D

3D CAD Renders

Fabric, garment and product renders plus the editable 3D scene, so colourways and variants can be re-rendered as the range grows.

Motion

Motion Assets

Drape animations, product turntables and showreels exported for presentation, e-commerce and social in the formats you need.
